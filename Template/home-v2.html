FAQ

Questions, answered with clarity

1. Can I customize Continer to fit my brand?
Yes, Continer is fully customizable. You can easily update colors, typography, layouts, images, and components to match your logistics or transportation brand identity.
2. Does it include CMS collections?
Yes, Continer includes CMS collections that make it easy to manage dynamic content such as services, blog posts, case studies, and other content directly from Webflow CMS.
3. Is it suitable for transport companies?
Yes, Continer is designed specifically for logistics, transportation, freight, shipping, delivery, and supply chain businesses. Its flexible layouts can also be adapted for related industries.
4. How difficult is it to edit layouts?
Continer is built with Webflow's visual editor, making it easy to edit layouts without coding. You can update sections, content, spacing, colors, and components directly in the Designer.
5. Does it include responsive design?
Yes, Continer is fully responsive and optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices, ensuring a consistent experience across different screen sizes.
6. Can I use it for multiple projects?
Yes, you can customize and reuse the template for multiple projects according to the applicable Webflow template license. Each project should comply with Webflow's licensing terms.
